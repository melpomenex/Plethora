Why energy changes the story

Illustrative reading passage

Complex cells contain specialized structures that turn nutrients into usable energy. Their internal organization lets different processes happen in parallel.

Understanding the relationship between energy and organization gives us a useful way to connect the details of cell biology. Each new structure changes what the cell can do.

Consider how a shared source of energy supports tasks across the cell, and how those tasks support one another.
