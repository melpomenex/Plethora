## 2. Strategic Integration Decisions

1. **Aggregate First via OS Health Hubs:** On mobile, **Apple HealthKit** and **Android Health Connect** cover Apple Watch, Galaxy Watch/Ring, RingConn, Garmin basic sleep, Polar, Withings, Suunto, and Ultrahuman without separate vendor partner approvals.
2. **Direct Cloud APIs Where Uniquely Valuable:** Implement first-party connectors for **Oura v2** and **WHOOP v2** (rich nocturnal HRV, Readiness/Recovery, nap markers, desktop-friendly sync without needing iOS/Android bridging), plus contract-complete approval-gated connectors for **Google Health API v4** (replacing retired Fitbit Web API) and **Garmin Commercial Health API** (never personal API).
3. **Never Pool Proprietary Scores or Incompatible HRV Metrics:** Oura Readiness (0–100), WHOOP Recovery (0–100%), and Garmin Body Battery are stored with explicit `provider_id` and `algorithm_id` and never averaged. Likewise, Apple Health `SDNN` and Health Connect/Oura/WHOOP `RMSSD` are tagged with `HrvMethod` (`sdnn` vs `rmssd`) and never merged in regression or trend charts.
