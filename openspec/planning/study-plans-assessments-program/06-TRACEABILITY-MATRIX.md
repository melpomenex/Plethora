# Study Plans and Assessments — Traceability Matrix

**Status:** normative planning and release-evidence map. No row asserts that production work or a test has completed. An unchecked owning task, failed assertion, unrun real-model/device activity, or unresolved architect item remains a **No-Go for the advertised scope**.  
**Requirement indexing:** `OBJ-01`–`OBJ-26` preserve the 26 original user-facing objectives. Slash-connected editor actions remain one objective (`OBJ-06`); deterministic and rubric grading remain one objective (`OBJ-18`) with separate proof obligations. `V-01`–`V-20` are mandatory architectural and verification concerns, not extra product objectives.  
**References:** `GS n` means step *n* in [the 30-step golden scenario](07-GOLDEN-SCENARIO.md); ranges are inclusive. `R-nn` means the corresponding [risk-register row](08-RISK-REGISTER.md), and `G0`–`G7` are its release gates. Evidence layers are **unit**, **native integration** (real temporary SQLite/repositories), **component**, **E2E** (reachable native UI using fake providers), and **manual qualification** (named real model or physical device). Manual evidence supplements rather than replaces automated assertions. Within every row, pure validators/algorithms are **unit**, SQLite/repository/API or fake-provider orchestration is **native integration**, UI evidence is **component**, numbered golden journeys are **E2E**, and real-provider/physical-device reviews are **manual qualification**; this assignment applies even when a dense row does not repeat each layer label beside every named case.
