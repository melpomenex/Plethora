## 1. Interpretation and gate policy

- **Likelihood** is a qualitative pre-control estimate (`Low`, `Medium`, `High`), not a measured probability. **Impact** is `Medium` (degraded utility), `High` (wrong learning outcome, lost work, or substantial unexpected cost), or `Critical` (privacy breach, irreversible data loss, scheduler corruption, or licensing/release blocker).
- Every risk is **open by default**. A checkbox, schema, table, isolated unit test, or fake-provider response is not sufficient closure evidence. Close or lower a risk only with the named evidence and the residual limitation still disclosed.
- **No-Go** applies to the named scope, not necessarily the whole application. For example, an unqualified essay provider must be blocked from automatic final essay grading while manual review and saved responses remain available.
- Ordinary verification uses deterministic fixtures/fake providers. Real-provider quality and physical-device behavior require separately named evidence; neither can be inferred from the other.
- Run A's conflict findings are historical evidence at the recorded baseline. Re-scan the active OpenSpec tree and migration head at implementation time rather than treating its counts or numeric migration suggestions as current.

### Release and change gates

| Gate | Scope that is No-Go until evidence exists |
|---|---|
| **G0 — admission** | Starting or accepting a dependency with an unresolved capability conflict, unverified implementation claim, or licensing concern, or without a fresh migration-head check. |
| **G1 — persistent plans** | Declaring C1 complete before native round-trip, DAG/source validation, safe legacy import, browser refusal, and navigation/settings integration evidence. |
| **G2 — generation execution** | Running or resuming generation without scoped evidence, contained tasks, destination/privacy/cost authorization, and lease/cancellation/idempotency tests. |
| **G3 — Ready paper** | Publishing or starting a paper with unresolved structural/source/key/rubric/quota errors or an unaccepted shortfall. Generated-paper *quality qualification* additionally needs the review/provider evidence in R-02, R-05, and R-06. |
| **G4 — attempts** | Claiming durable/offline exam sessions before response-loss, double-submit, restart, mobile-lifecycle, timer, and public-payload tests pass. |
| **G5 — official subjective grades** | Marking an AI subjective grade Final without structural/consistency validation, or advertising a provider for rubric grading without real-model evaluation. Manual review remains the fallback. |
| **G6 — evidence/remediation** | Publishing evidence levels, gaps, diagnostics, or remediation that fail independence/repetition/correction fixtures or mutate Review, Queue, cards, or FSRS implicitly. |
| **G7 — release claims** | Claiming end-to-end, accessibility/e-ink, performance, backup, platform, privacy, or provider readiness without C8 evidence. Unsupported routes must be disabled or explicitly labelled, not silently presented as ready. |

## 6. Evidence acceptance rules

1. **Attach evidence at the lowest useful gate.** Do not defer all structural checks to C8; C8 independently exercises integration and qualification after owning changes pass.
2. **Separate negative from positive evidence.** A valid fixture passing does not show rejection of forged handles, injected content, stale CAS, duplicate submission, revoked consent, or unsupported platforms; each needs explicit negative/fault-injection coverage.
3. **Separate structural validity from quality.** Schema/gradeability/source checks can permit a structurally valid artifact. They cannot qualify a provider's curriculum, distractors, rubric grading, accessibility, or educational claims.
4. **Preserve unresolved states honestly.** Needs Review, Grading Failed, insufficient evidence, stale/missing source, quota shortfall, unsupported platform, and unqualified provider are valid safe outcomes. Converting them into Success, zero, Complete, or certainty to close a test is itself a release blocker.
5. **Record limitations with the result.** Every closed gate links the test/device/provider/version/commit and restates the applicable residual limitation above. Changes to provider, model, source renderer, scheduler, storage, or evidence policy reopen the affected evidence rather than inheriting a blanket pass.
