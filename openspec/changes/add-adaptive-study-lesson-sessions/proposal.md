## Why

Study Plan topics can open a scoped tutor from remediation, but learners lack a persistent lesson that can start from a plan objective and resume at the same question. Optional diagnostics should shape a reviewed curriculum rather than interrupt creation or silently alter the published path.

## What Changes

- Add a resumable lesson tied to a plan version and objective, with Guided Socratic, Explain + Check, and Challenge Me styles.
- Present one focal question with reasoning input, bounded hints, exact source navigation, Skip, and a direct explanation action. Hand off independent checks separately.
- Integrate the optional diagnostic into preview, reusing existing generation, assessment, and draft adaptation.
- Apply the versioned source policy, eligibility, and endpoint consent to every lesson action and retry. Tutor dialogue stays activity, with no automatic grades or scheduler writes.
- Builds on `add-study-plan-knowledge-policies` (PRD SP-07–12 and SP-17). Plan Home, sustained practice, and spaced review are specified separately.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `adaptive-study-lesson-sessions`: Durable topic lessons, teaching variants, and optional diagnostic entry and adaptation.

### Modified Capabilities

- `ai-socratic-tutoring`: Apply per-version authority to Study Plan tutoring while retaining existing document and selection behavior.

## Impact

Study Plan routes and preview, TutorSession/TutorSheet, native session persistence, diagnostic components, assessment handoff, source navigation, and desktop/mobile accessibility.
