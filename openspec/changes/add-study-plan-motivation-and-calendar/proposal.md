## Why

A learner preparing for an exam needs to see whether the remaining lessons and due reviews fit the time available. Optional recognition can make real learning progress visible without rewarding chat volume or inflating demonstrated evidence.

## What Changes

- Add an optional exam deadline and a planning view that separates remaining lessons from projected due reviews and honors rest-day preferences.
- Offer optional next-step quests, evidence-backed achievements, a quiet session wrap-up, and flexible weekly cadence.
- Keep recognition dismissible and non-punitive. Measure engagement separately from delayed independent transfer.
- Builds on `integrate-study-plan-home-and-practice` (PRD SP-18–19). No automatic rescheduling or competitive ranking.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `study-plan-motivation-and-calendar`: Deadline workload projection and optional recognition derived from study events.

### Modified Capabilities

## Impact

Plan Home and per-plan preferences, existing workload forecast, Review and assessment read models, accessibility, and local product metrics.
