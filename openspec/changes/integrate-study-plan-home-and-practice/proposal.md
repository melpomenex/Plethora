## Why

Learners can create plans, take assessments, and review cards, but need one place to see what to do next, what evidence supports that recommendation, and what is due. A weak result should lead back to the right lesson, then to fresh independent practice.

## What Changes

- Add a Plan Home and Today entry with one Continue action, a reason, path and source coverage, actual due reviews, and evidence-backed focus areas.
- Route reader selections, library collections/tags, plan cards, and weak results into the appropriate creation or lesson context.
- Connect scoped practice, remediation, fresh reassessment, and explicit review-card proposals to the existing assessment and Review/FSRS systems.
- Keep coverage, activity, and demonstrated evidence separate; show assistance, repetition, and unverified provenance where relevant.
- Builds on the knowledge-policy and lesson-session changes (PRD SP-07, SP-09, SP-12–15, SP-17). Deadline projections and achievements are separate.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `study-plan-home-and-practice`: Plan recommendations, daily navigation, contextual practice/remediation, and plan-linked review.

### Modified Capabilities

- `knowledge-gap-detection`: Route Study Plan assessment gaps to versioned lessons and targeted reassessment with return context.

## Impact

Study Plans tab and editor, reader/library actions, study evidence, assessment navigation, Learn This/card proposals, and existing Queue, Review, and FSRS adapters.
