## MODIFIED Requirements

### Requirement: Horizontal wheel/trackpad scrolling of the tab strip
When the pointer is over the horizontally-overflowing tab strip, the system SHALL scroll the tabs horizontally in response to: (a) native horizontal wheel deltas (`deltaX`, e.g. trackpad two-finger horizontal, high-resolution trackpads), (b) Shift+wheel (which conventionally maps to horizontal scrolling), and (c) vertical wheel deltas translated to horizontal tab scrolling while hovering/focusing the strip. The existing `handleTabWheel`/capture-listener mechanism in `TabBar.tsx` SHALL be extended, not replaced with per-screen handlers.

#### Scenario: Trackpad horizontal gesture scrolls tabs
- **WHEN** the user performs a horizontal two-finger trackpad gesture over an overflowed tab strip
- **THEN** the strip SHALL scroll horizontally in the direction of the gesture

#### Scenario: Shift+wheel scrolls tabs horizontally
- **WHEN** the user presses Shift and rolls the mouse wheel over an overflowed tab strip
- **THEN** the strip SHALL scroll horizontally (via the resulting `deltaX`) in the conventional direction

#### Scenario: Vertical wheel over the strip translates to horizontal
- **WHEN** the user rolls a conventional mouse wheel (vertical delta) while the pointer is over an overflowed tab strip
- **THEN** the strip SHALL scroll horizontally by the delta, and the page/reader SHALL NOT scroll

### Requirement: Scrolling the strip never changes the active tab or scrolls tab content
Wheel/trackpad scrolling over the strip SHALL only scroll the strip. It SHALL NOT activate a tab, close a tab, or scroll the content of the active tab.

#### Scenario: Scroll does not switch tabs
- **WHEN** the user scrolls the overflowed strip with the wheel
- **THEN** the active tab SHALL remain unchanged

#### Scenario: Content does not scroll
- **WHEN** the user scrolls the strip with the wheel
- **THEN** the content pane SHALL NOT scroll

### Requirement: Page scroll is not trapped at strip boundaries
When the tab strip has reached its scroll boundary (fully scrolled left or right and there is no more overflow in the gesture direction), the system SHALL NOT block the page-level vertical scroll where reasonable.

#### Scenario: Boundary does not trap page scroll
- **WHEN** the strip is at a boundary and the user continues to scroll vertically over it
- **THEN** the page SHALL scroll normally (the strip SHALL release the gesture rather than consuming it with no effect)

### Requirement: Momentum/inertial scroll works across platforms
Inertial/momentum scroll generated by high-resolution trackpads SHALL continue to work on Linux, macOS, and Windows, consistent with the existing WebKitGTK-aware listener approach.

#### Scenario: Inertial scrolling continues
- **WHEN** a trackpad emits a burst of small deltas after a flick over the strip
- **THEN** the strip SHALL keep scrolling smoothly with the momentum and SHALL not stutter or reverse direction
