## MODIFIED Requirements

### Requirement: Actions route to concrete remediation

Each gap SHALL offer Explain/Open Source, Teach me, Find/choose material where evidence is missing, Generate Practice, Make Cards, Follow-up Exam/Plan Adjustment when relevant, and Dismiss. Actions SHALL open with the gap's concept, validated evidence, objectives, misconception and related sources through existing reader, source-scoped tutor, assessment builder, Learn This proposal and Study Plan draft owners. Only controls usable with the current provider/source/native capability SHALL appear enabled. Recommendation disposition SHALL persist, and actions SHALL remain optional without automatic cloud work, curriculum publication, card creation or Queue/FSRS mutation.

#### Scenario: Teach-me carries context

- **WHEN** the user taps Teach me on a gap
- **THEN** the tutor opens pre-loaded with the gap's concept, evidence summary and related sources and its retrieval is explicitly filtered to the validated source documents

#### Scenario: Full remediation loop is reachable

- **WHEN** a learner identifies a weak assessed concept from results
- **THEN** they can open the exact source, study/practice with TutorSheet/hints, optionally approve card proposals and generate/retake a follow-up assessment without manually reconstructing its topic/context

#### Scenario: Rejected remediation stays non-destructive

- **WHEN** the user dismisses the gap/recommendation or closes a proposal without accepting
- **THEN** active surfaces honor dismissal under the evidence-cutoff rule and no source, plan, card, review or Queue/FSRS state changes
