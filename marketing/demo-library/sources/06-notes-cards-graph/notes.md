# Reader notes (original, CC0-1.0)

These notes belong to the marketing story **Why we forget what we read.** They are fictional study notes, not a real person’s journal. No emails, phone numbers, or photographs.

## After the essay

Highlighting is a queue at best. If I do not extract a question, the yellow is a souvenir.

Fluency while the page is open is not evidence I can produce the claim tomorrow.

## After the lecture

Overnight replay prefers effort. A comfortable reread does not earn a second pass during sleep.

Post-lecture ritual: three answered questions, one unanswered, retrieve in the morning.

## After James

Hooks, not a native faculty. Association is the retrieval cue. Isolated sentences die.

Habit as fly-wheel: make the retrieve-then-check loop automatic.
